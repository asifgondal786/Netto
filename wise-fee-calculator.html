Frequently asked questions

Does Wise have one fixed transfer fee?

No single fee applies to every transfer. The quote can depend on currency pair, amount, funding method, destination, and current pricing.

How can I estimate a Wise transfer with Netto?

Use the Custom gateway and enter the percentage and any fixed charge from your Wise quote. Netto then estimates the remainder using a simple percentage-plus-fixed-fee model.

Does Netto calculate exchange rates?

No. Netto does not convert currencies or fetch live exchange rates. It only estimates fees in the calculator's selected currency.
