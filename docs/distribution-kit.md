## Short description

Netto is a free payment fee calculator for sellers and freelancers. Estimate net payouts after modeled fees, compare gateways, or solve for a price that reaches a target amount.

## Product listing

**Tagline:** Know what you keep after payment fees.

**Description:** Compare illustrative Stripe, PayPal, Etsy, Shopify Payments, Square, eBay, and Gumroad fee models. Netto calculates an estimated payout from a sale amount or works backward from a target net. It also supports custom percentage and fixed fees for contract or transfer estimates. Rates are not live-verified; always confirm current provider pricing.
