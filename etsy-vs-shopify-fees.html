Frequently asked questions

Are Etsy and Shopify fees directly comparable?

Not completely. Etsy can include marketplace-related fee components, while the Shopify Payments estimate models online payment processing. Store subscriptions, services, advertising, taxes, and other costs are not included.

Can I compare both modeled fees on one sale?

Yes. The comparison calculator shows the current illustrative Etsy and Shopify Payments models side by side for the same entered amount.

Are these current official Etsy and Shopify prices?

No. Netto uses approximate comparison rates that are not live-verified. Check your marketplace, store plan, location, and current provider pricing before making decisions.
