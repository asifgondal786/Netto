What this Shopify estimate includes

The comparison model covers an illustrative online card-processing fee. It does not include Shopify subscription plans, third-party transaction charges, apps, taxes, refunds, or every payment method. Your store's plan, country, and account terms may produce different rates.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include Shopify plan fees?

No. Netto estimates transaction processing only. Shopify subscription charges, third-party transaction charges, taxes, and other plan costs are not included.

Can Shopify Payments rates vary?

Yes. Actual rates can depend on plan, country, payment method, and account terms. Check the pricing shown for your store.

Can I compare Shopify Payments with Etsy?

Yes. Netto has an Etsy-versus-Shopify comparison guide and an A/B calculator using the current illustrative fee models.
