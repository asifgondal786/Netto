How the payment fee calculator works

In forward mode, Netto subtracts the estimated percentage and fixed fees from your sale price. In reverse mode, it solves for the charge that leaves your chosen target after those fees. Add cost of goods to see it alongside the payout, and set monthly orders to understand the transaction context.

Estimated fee = sale price × percentage rate + fixed per-order fees. Some providers apply additional or tiered fees, taxes, marketplace charges, or plan-specific pricing that this simple estimate may not include.

Payment fee calculator questions

How do I calculate my net payout after payment fees?

Enter the sale amount and select a payment gateway. Netto estimates the percentage and fixed fees, then shows the remaining payout. Add applicable international or currency-conversion charges when relevant.

How can I price a product to receive a target amount?

Choose Reverse mode and enter the amount you want to keep. The calculator solves for a sale price that covers the selected gateway's modeled fees and leaves that target net.

Are the payment processor fees exact and up to date?

No. Netto's built-in rates are estimates for comparison, not live quotes. Provider fees can change and depend on location, account, product, plan, card type, and transaction details. Confirm the current fee with your provider before making pricing or financial decisions.

Why is there a fixed fee as well as a percentage?

Many payment providers charge both a proportion of the transaction and a fixed amount per successful payment. The fixed portion has a larger impact on smaller orders, which is why comparing only percentage rates can be misleading.
