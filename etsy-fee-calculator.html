What Etsy fees may affect a sale?

Etsy sellers may encounter transaction fees, payment-processing fees, and listing fees. Other charges can apply depending on location, advertising, currency conversion, regulatory requirements, and account activity. The specific amount and basis can change, so treat Netto's breakdown as an illustrative comparison rather than an Etsy statement.

How to estimate Etsy fees

Choose Etsy and enter the sale price to view the modeled fee breakdown and amount remaining. Use Reverse mode to estimate a price for a target payout. For planning profit rather than payout alone, include your product cost in the calculator inputs and compare the same sale across gateways.

Netto's default values are not live-verified and may not include every Etsy fee or seller-specific adjustment. Check Etsy's current fee policy and your shop payment account before setting prices.

Frequently asked questions

Does Etsy charge more than a payment-processing fee?

Etsy sellers may have multiple fee types, such as transaction, payment-processing, and listing fees. Other charges may apply based on services, location, and activity.

Are Etsy fees the same in every country?

No. Rates and fee rules can vary by seller location, currency, payment processing availability, and applicable regulations. Check the fee policy that applies to your shop.

Is this an official Etsy fee calculator?

No. Netto is an independent estimation tool. Its example rates are not live-verified and should not replace Etsy's current fee policy or your shop's transaction records.
