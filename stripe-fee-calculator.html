Estimate how much of a Stripe payment you keep after processing fees, or work backwards to the price that reaches your target payout.

For a quick estimate, enter a sale amount in Netto's calculator and choose Stripe. The result shows the modeled fee components and estimated net payout. Select Reverse when you know the amount you want to receive and need to estimate what to charge.

What can affect a Stripe payment fee?

Payment costs may include a percentage of the transaction and a fixed charge. Card origin, currency conversion, payment method, account location, and your Stripe pricing agreement can change the final amount. Netto includes optional international-card and currency-conversion adjustments in its comparison model; check your Stripe dashboard and current pricing for the rate that applies to your account.

How to estimate your Stripe net payout

  1. Enter your customer's sale amount.
  2. Choose Stripe and enable any applicable international or conversion adjustments.
  3. Read the fee breakdown and estimated amount you keep.
  4. Use Reverse mode to estimate the charge needed to reach a target net.

The basic estimate is sale amount multiplied by the percentage rate, plus fixed transaction charges. Refunds, disputes, taxes, subscriptions, platform fees, and custom pricing may not be included.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official Stripe calculator?

No. Netto is an independent estimation tool. It does not access your Stripe account or provide an official quote.

Why does my Stripe payout differ from the estimate?

Your account's location and pricing, card type, currency, refunds, disputes, taxes, and other adjustments can affect settlement. Review the transaction details in your Stripe dashboard.

Are Stripe fees live-verified here?

No. Rates are editable estimates for comparison and may not match your current contract. Confirm applicable fees on Stripe's pricing page and in your account.
