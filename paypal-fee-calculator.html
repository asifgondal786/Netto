PayPal fees depend on the transaction

The rate can vary with the PayPal service, transaction type, seller location, buyer location, currency, and your account terms. A payment fee may combine a percentage with a fixed amount; international or currency-conversion charges may also apply. Netto uses a default comparison estimate, so check your account's current fee schedule for an exact figure.

Estimate a PayPal fee or target price

In Forward mode, enter the amount charged to the customer to estimate what remains after modeled fees. In Reverse mode, enter the amount you want to keep and Netto solves for a charge that covers the selected percentage and fixed fee. Compare the estimate with Stripe, Etsy, Square, Shopify Payments, eBay, or Gumroad in the main calculator.

The simple model does not necessarily include refunds, disputes, taxes, seller-specific rates, or every PayPal product fee.

Frequently asked questions

Does PayPal charge a percentage and a fixed fee?

Many PayPal commercial transactions use a percentage-based charge and a fixed fee, but the exact pricing depends on transaction and account details. Verify the applicable schedule for your account.

Can this calculator tell me the exact PayPal payout?

No. It provides an estimate using a default fee model. Your actual payout can reflect location, currency, account terms, transaction category, and other adjustments.

Can I calculate a price that nets a specific amount?

Yes. Choose Reverse mode in the calculator, enter your target payout, and Netto estimates the charge required under the selected fee assumptions.
