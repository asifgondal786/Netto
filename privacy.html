Netto payment fee calculators

Privacy

Netto calculates fee estimates in your browser. Your calculator inputs are not sent to Netto's server.

Share links

If you copy a share link, the calculator settings such as sale amount, selected gateway, and mode are encoded in that URL. Anyone you share the link with can see those settings.

Analytics

Netto uses Plausible Analytics for aggregate pageview and interaction counts. Calculator input events include only the field name, never the entered amount. Share-link events do not include the copied URL. Pageview URLs omit query strings so shared calculator values are not included in analytics.

Plausible Analytics is a third-party service. See the Plausible data policy for how it handles analytics requests.

Theme preference

Your light or dark theme preference is stored in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to the site.
