What to include in a freelancer payout estimate

Use the service fee shown for the specific contract. Keep withdrawal charges, currency conversion, taxes, and optional services separate unless you have a reliable effective fee to enter. The result is a planning estimate and is not connected to your Upwork account.

Frequently asked questions

Does Netto use a fixed Upwork fee rate?

No. Upwork fees can depend on contract terms, so this guide does not assume a platform-wide rate. Enter the applicable service fee shown in your account using the Custom gateway.

How do I estimate my Upwork take-home amount?

Enter the contract amount, select Custom, and replace the default percentage and fixed fee with the charges that apply to your contract. The result is an estimate, not an Upwork statement.

Does the estimate include withdrawal or currency costs?

No. Withdrawal methods, currency conversion, taxes, and other account charges can affect your final amount and are not automatically modeled.
