Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Stripe or PayPal?

It depends on the transaction and the rates that apply to your accounts. Netto compares illustrative percentage and fixed-fee models; it is not a live quote from either provider.

Can I compare both providers on the same sale amount?

Yes. Open the comparison calculator, enter one sale amount, and review the estimated fees and net payouts side by side.

Does the comparison include every Stripe and PayPal fee?

No. Provider products, account agreements, card types, international transactions, refunds, and other charges can change the total. Check your current account pricing.
